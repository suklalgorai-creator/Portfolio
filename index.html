01 • My Story

From NEET Aspirant to Shipping Software

Discipline forged in competitive exam prep, channeled into building products people actually use

I'm Suklal Gorai, a first-year B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Netaji Subhas University, Jamshedpur.
